1
Reads your words

Open models read "Parle G 800g" or "ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಸೋಪು" the way shops write them, and pick the HSN heading and the matching notification entry.

2
Rates come from the law

The model never states a rate. Code reads it from CBIC Notifications 9/2025 and 10/2025, with amendments up to 1 May 2026.

3
Asks when it depends

Loose or pre-packed? Above Rs 2,500? When the rate depends on a fact the bill doesn't show, BillSahi asks instead of guessing.

BillSahi is a helper, not tax advice. Confirm anything marked "ask" with your accountant. Open source on GitHub.
