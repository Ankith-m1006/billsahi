Is your supplier's bill sahi?

Photograph a supplier's bill. BillSahi checks every line against the official GST rate, tells you whether the bill is good enough to claim the GST back, warns you when a supplier charges more than you paid before, and keeps count of what you owe each supplier.

Every bill you check is kept here. BillSahi adds it to what you owe that supplier and remembers what you paid for each item.

How the check works

  1. It reads shop language. Brands, abbreviations, Hinglish, Hindi or Kannada: "Parle G 800g", "ಬಟ್ಟೆ ಸೋಪು 4pc", "hawai chappal 199". Open models pick the 4-digit HSN heading.
  2. It picks the entry, then reads the law. One heading can hold several rates (biscuits 5%, bread 0%). The model picks the matching entry of CBIC Notification 9/2025 or 10/2025; code reads the rate from it.
  3. Arithmetic stays arithmetic. The Rs 2,500 line for clothes and footwear, and the rupee difference on every bill line, are calculated by code, not by a model.
  4. It asks when it can't know. Loose rice is exempt, pre-packed rice is 5%. If the bill doesn't say, you get a question, not a guess.
